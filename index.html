[ 003 / OUR STORY ]

The right resources, at the right time.

CAMI Consultancy was founded by Dr Mallika Bohm — an expert in protective coating technologies for metals, corrosion control, and surface engineering. Her PhD in Condensed Matter Physics from UCL, followed by work at Unilever Research, Tata Steel Europe & India, and Talga Technologies, made one thing clear: companies kept lacking the right resources at the right time.

Without the proper expertise when it was required, organisations wasted time and money, failed to resolve problems, and lost their competitive edge. So CAMI was formed — drawing on experts from multiple industries and backgrounds to deliver game-changing technical solutions, independently and confidentially.
