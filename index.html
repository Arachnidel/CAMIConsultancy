[ 003 / OUR STORY ]

The right resources, at the right time.

CAMI Consultancy was founded by Dr Mallika Bohm — an expert in protective coating technologies for metals, corrosion control, and surface engineering. Her PhD in Condensed Matter Physics from UCL, followed by work at Unilever Research, Tata Steel Europe & India, and Talga Technologies, made one thing clear: companies kept lacking the right resources at the right time.

Without the proper expertise when it was required, organisations wasted time and money, failed to resolve problems, and lost their competitive edge. So CAMI was formed — drawing on experts from multiple industries and backgrounds to deliver game-changing technical solutions, independently and confidentially.

Alongside Dr Mallika Bohm, Dr Sivasambu Bohm serves as Chief Scientific Officer — bringing 25 years of graphene, carbon nanotube and advanced carbon expertise from Tata Steel, Talga Advanced Materials, IIT Bombay, the Cambridge Graphene Centre and Imperial College London. Together they cover the full arc from protective coatings and corrosion engineering to the most advanced carbon nanomaterials in commercial production.

CSO
Chief Scientific Officer

Dr Sivasambu Bohm

Graphene · Carbon nanotubes · Advanced carbon · Conductive inks · Energy storage

An internationally recognised authority on graphene, carbon nanotubes and advanced carbon materials, Dr Bohm has spent 25 years taking these materials from the laboratory to commercial production. His career spans senior technical and leadership roles at Tata Steel R&D, Talga Advanced Materials (CTO), IIT Bombay, the Cambridge Graphene Centre and Imperial College London, where he was a Royal Society Industry Fellow.

Carbon Nanotubes & Dispersion

Dr Bohm is a leading industrial voice on carbon nanotube dispersion — the single greatest barrier to CNT commercialisation. He advises on the selection, optimisation and scale-up of SWCNT and MWCNT dispersion processes, balancing performance against tube damage, throughput and environmental impact. His expertise spans conventional and emerging routes — high-shear, microfluidisation, milling and green-chemistry formulations — and he has led industrial implementation of CNT and graphene dispersions in real products.

Graphene & Advanced Carbon

From graphene synthesis and functionalisation to conductive inks, anti-corrosion coatings and battery anode materials, Dr Bohm has delivered multiple commercial products across the energy, transport, construction and electronics sectors. He is equally at home in the lab and on the production line — translating novel carbon materials into repeatable, manufacturable technology.

  • Education: PhD, Electrochemistry — University of Bath; MSc & BSc Chemical Engineering — TFH Berlin
  • Recognition: Royal Society Industry Fellow · FRSC · C.Chem
  • Output: 12 granted patents · 45+ applications · 100+ publications · multiple industrial products launched
